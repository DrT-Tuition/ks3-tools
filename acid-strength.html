Each test: 10 cm³ of dilute hydrochloric acid and 5 drops of universal indicator. The tablet is crushed. Add it bit by bit and stir. In your stomach, a tablet only neutralises the extra acid: your stomach still needs some acid.

Universal indicator colour chart (pH 0 to 14)

Model limits: Real acids have billions of particles: here a few stand for them all. Water particles are not drawn. Every acid is drawn the same way, with one H part and one A part. In a weak acid, 1 in 4 particles split up here: that share, and the numbers of particles, are set by the model. The pH is a whole number worked out by the model from how many H particles have split up in the same volume. Real pH values vary. Brand A and brand B are made-up tablets: their amounts are model values. Never taste or touch acids in the lab.
