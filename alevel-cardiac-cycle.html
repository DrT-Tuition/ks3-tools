      About this model

      The graphs are for the left side of the heart. The curves are smooth model curves with typical values for a healthy adult (pressures in kPa; 1 kPa is about 7.5 mmHg). The right side of the heart has the same timing but much lower pressures. In a real heart, blood keeps moving out for a moment after the ventricle pressure drops below the aortic pressure. The heart drawing is a simplified front view. OCR’s specification names the SAN, AVN and Purkyne tissue; the bundle of His is the usual name for the conducting fibres in the septum. The ECG traces are drawn on standard paper (25 mm s⁻¹, 10 mm mV⁻¹) and are simplified; real traces are noisier.
