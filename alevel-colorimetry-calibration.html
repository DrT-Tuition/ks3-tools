About this model

All the numbers are invented example data, with a little random scatter, and the colours are idealised. Real Benedict’s colours and absorbances depend on the reagent, the volumes, how long and how hot the tubes are heated, and the colorimeter. The time in the water bath is speeded up. The standards here are made by proportional dilution from a 25.0 mmol dm⁻³ stock; the serial dilution is shown for comparison only. Sample U is urine; sports drink J contains no sugar other than glucose, and was diluted 1 in 20 before the test. The biosensor is a simplified outline: most home meters use a mediator rather than detecting the product directly, and they are calibrated by the maker. The pipette scale, the colorimeter and the sensor are drawn in a simple style and not to scale.
