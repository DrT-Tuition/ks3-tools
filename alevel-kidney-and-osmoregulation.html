About this model

The values are typical, rounded textbook values for both kidneys together (a single nephron makes only about a ten-millionth of this). The nephron is drawn much bigger than life and simplified: the loop and collecting duct are straight, and the capillaries round the tubule (and the vasa recta round the loop) are not shown. In the Nephron graph the urea amount stays the same after the PCT; in real kidneys some urea also moves between the collecting duct and the loop, which helps to concentrate the medulla. The loop of Henle values are arbitrary units of solute concentration (blood plasma = 300); the model uses a fixed difference of 200 units between the limbs at each level. ADH also acts on the DCT; here only the collecting duct is shown. The dialysis curves are a simple model (the urea concentration falls by the same fraction each hour). The liver makes urea from excess amino acids (deamination and the ornithine cycle); that is not modelled here.
