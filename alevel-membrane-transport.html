About this model

The pictures are schematic and not to scale: a real membrane is about 7 nm thick, and the lipids and proteins are far more crowded than shown. In the Transport view only one substance is shown at a time, and each particle stands for a great many molecules. The rate graphs are initial rates in arbitrary units, as if there were none of the substance inside the cell at the start. In the practical all the absorbances are invented example data with a little random scatter; real values depend on the beetroot, the size of the discs, the time, the volume of liquid and the colorimeter. The discs are rinsed first to wash away pigment from cells cut open when they were prepared. The red pigment (betalain) is stored in the vacuoles. Osmosis is in the Water Potential tool.
