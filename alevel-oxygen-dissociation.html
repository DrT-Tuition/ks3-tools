    About this model

    The haemoglobin drawing is schematic: the real change of shape when O₂ binds is a small rotation of the chains, and the meter only shows the trend. The curves come from the Hill equation (saturation = 100 × pO₂ⁿ ÷ (P₅₀ⁿ + pO₂ⁿ)), with positions and shapes close to real blood; real curves also change with temperature and pH. Myoglobin, llama and lugworm curves go beyond the OCR specification; they are approximate, for practice with data questions. The red blood cell shows the main reactions only. The percentages for CO₂ carriage are typical values.
