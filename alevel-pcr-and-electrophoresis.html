    About this model

    PCR: the drawing shows a 1000 bp piece of DNA; a real sample is a whole genome, many millions of base pairs long. The temperatures are typical values; real ones depend on the primers used. The model assumes every molecule is copied in every cycle; in a real run fewer are, and copying slows after about 30 cycles as the primers and nucleotides run low. Gel: each band’s distance comes from a simple model of a 1% agarose gel run for 40 minutes; a real gel’s bands are fuzzier. The profiles show four fragments each, much simplified: real DNA profiles compare many regions that vary between people. School gels are often stained with a blue dye; labs use fluorescent dyes and UV light. Proteins can be separated in the same way, once they have been given an even negative charge.
