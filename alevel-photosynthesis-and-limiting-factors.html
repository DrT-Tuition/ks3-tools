About this model

The chloroplast and cell pictures are drawn, not photographs; a real chloroplast is about 5 µm long and has many more stacks of membranes inside. The membrane in the Light-dependent stage view is schematic: the electron carriers are shown as a few blobs, and the numbers of electrons and H⁺ ions are not to scale. The Calvin cycle levels come from a simple computer model (arbitrary units): it shows the right directions of change, not real concentrations. The limiting-factor curves are example data (arbitrary units) for one plant; real curves depend on the species and the conditions. The rates are gross photosynthesis, with respiration ignored.
