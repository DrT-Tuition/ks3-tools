About this model

Each tube holds 5.0 g of organisms (or 5.0 g of glass beads in the control) above 5 g of soda lime, behind a gauze; the capillary tube has a bore of 1.0 mm (radius 0.5 mm). The readings are steady, idealised values: real respirometers drift, and the organisms take a few minutes to settle after handling. The control tube cancels changes in the temperature and pressure of the air, so only the organisms move the fluid. With soda lime the fluid shows the O₂ used; without it the fluid shows O₂ used minus CO₂ given out. The RQ values (carbohydrate 1.0, protein about 0.9, lipid about 0.7) are for complete aerobic respiration of one substrate; a real organism often respires a mixture, so its RQ lies in between. Maggots should be handled gently and returned to their culture afterwards; soda lime is corrosive (wear eye protection and gloves).
