      About this model

      Population: one gene with two alleles (A, dominant, for dark fur; a for pale fur), in pocket mice, which really do have a dominant allele for dark fur. Each generation, selection acts on the parents (strong: the disadvantaged phenotype is half as likely to breed; weak: 20 % less likely), 10 % of the gene pool comes from migrants with A at 0.5 when migration is on, and the offspring get two alleles at random from the parents’ gene pool. Seeded random numbers; mutation is not modelled; generations don’t overlap. A bottleneck leaves 4 random survivors; a founder group is 5 mice, and the mainland carries on (dashed line). Types of selection: idealised normal curves for 1000 birds, not a simulation. Two of the four types are named in OCR’s guidance, and artificial selection is in OCR 6.1.2(h); the fourth goes beyond the specification. The speciation steps are simplified; the apple maggot fly is a real example of speciation that may be happening now.
