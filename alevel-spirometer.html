About this model

The drawings are simplified: real airways branch about 23 times, and the cross-sections are not to scale with each other. The spirometer trace shows the volume of gas in the spirometer chamber, so the pen goes down when the person breathes in. Some spirometers and books draw it the other way up; the volumes you read are the same. The traces use typical values for a young adult and are made smooth: real breaths are less regular, and breathing in is a little quicker than breathing out. At the end of each breath the pressure in the lungs is back to atmospheric pressure, so the air stops moving. The fish graph shows the oxygen saturation of water and blood along one gill lamella, in relative units.
