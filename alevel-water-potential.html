About this model

The cylinders are left overnight (18 hours), so the tissue comes close to equilibrium with each solution. The masses and percentage changes are invented example data with random scatter (about ±1 %) and the odd anomaly. The cell pictures and particle close-ups are schematic and not to scale: real solutions have far more water molecules than solute particles. The cells' water potentials (about −700 kPa) and the conversion table (sucrose solutions at about 20 °C) are typical textbook values. A real plant cell in pure water reaches equilibrium when the pressure potential balances its solute potential; the model shows the end state only. In the experiment the cylinders are all cut 3.0 cm long with one cork borer, so their surface area to volume ratios match.
