What the atoms look like

Count the atoms

Use your own equation

Type formulas with plain numbers, like H2O. Put + between substances and -> between the two sides.

Model limits: real atoms are far too small to see, and they have no colour. Metals, carbon, and compounds such as magnesium oxide and sodium chloride are giant structures, not separate molecules. A dashed box shows one formula's worth of atoms, not a molecule. Water, oxygen, carbon dioxide and methane really are separate molecules. State symbols are left out.

Remember: the small numbers are part of the formula. Change them and you make a different substance. H2O is water; H2O2 is hydrogen peroxide. In a reaction, atoms are only rearranged. None are made or lost. If a gas escapes, its atoms are still there, in the air.
