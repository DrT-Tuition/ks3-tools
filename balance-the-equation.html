About this model

real atoms are far too small to see, and they have no colour. Metals, carbon, and compounds such as magnesium oxide and sodium chloride are giant structures, not separate molecules. A dashed box shows one formula's worth of atoms, not a molecule. Water, oxygen, carbon dioxide and methane really are separate molecules. State symbols are left out.

Remember: the small numbers are part of the formula. Change them and you make a different substance. H2O is water; H2O2 is hydrogen peroxide. In a reaction, atoms are only rearranged. None are made or lost. If a gas escapes, its atoms are still there, in the air.
