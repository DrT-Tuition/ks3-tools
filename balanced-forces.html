Does the speed change? The forces are:

Model limits: All forces act along one line: left and right, or up and down. Speeds are not to scale. Normally the resistive force stays as you set it at every speed; turn on Real road to make air resistance grow as the car speeds up. A resistive force can stop the car, but never makes it go backwards. The up and down arrows on the car use a smaller scale than the left and right arrows. In the forces circus, the 100 g masses weigh 1 N; the other sizes are the model's own numbers, and the falling and moving parts are shown in slow motion.
