This is mixing light, not paint. Light colours add together.

Make your prediction, then press Check to switch on the light.

Model limits: This model builds every colour from just red, green and blue. Real white light holds every colour of the rainbow. Real filters and dyes let through or reflect bands of colours, not exactly three. How your eyes and brain see colour is more complicated.
