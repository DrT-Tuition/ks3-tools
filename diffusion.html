Feedback

Model limits: Real particles are far too small to see, and there are billions of them. Here each dot stands for lots of particles. The clock is model time: real gas particles move much faster, and real dye takes many minutes to spread. In the gas, the hot–cold speed difference is made bigger so you can see it. Water particles are not drawn. Air and water are perfectly still here. Only gases and liquids are shown.
