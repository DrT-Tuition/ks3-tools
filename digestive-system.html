    Model limits: The organs are simplified and not to scale. Real food takes hours to pass through, not seconds. Each particle stands for one small molecule, far too small to see. Real starch, protein and fat molecules are much bigger. Real enzymes and food molecules are 3D and much smaller, and their shapes are simplified here. The Enzymes scene shows the inside of the small intestine. Its wall and villi are simplified.
