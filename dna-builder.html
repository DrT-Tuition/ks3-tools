Purines have a double ring: adenine (A) and guanine (G).

Pyrimidines have a single ring: cytosine (C), thymine (T) and uracil (U).

Memory hook: PURe As Gold (purines are adenine and guanine). Then cut the rest: Cytosine, Uracil, Thymine (C, U, T).

Pairing rules: adenine pairs with thymine (A–T, 2 hydrogen bonds). Guanine pairs with cytosine (G–C, 3 hydrogen bonds).

Hydrogen bonds are drawn as dashes between the bases.
