PhosphateDeoxyribose sugarBases: purines (A, G) double ring, pyrimidines (C, T) single ringExtra phosphate groups (activated nucleotide)Original strand: solidNew strand: hatched

Purines have a double ring: adenine (A) and guanine (G).

Pyrimidines have a single ring: cytosine (C) and thymine (T).

Complementary base pairing: A pairs only with T (2 hydrogen bonds); C pairs only with G (3 hydrogen bonds).

So each template base allows only one partner.

Model limits: The molecule is far shorter than real DNA. The real helix is 3D; here it is a flat wave, and a flat ladder once untwisted. Real error rates (about 1 in 10⁸) are after proofreading. Enzyme shapes and band positions are schematic, and everything is slowed down.
