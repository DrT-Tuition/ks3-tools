Change

Model limits: Real food webs have many more species and links. Animals can switch to other foods, and the weather, disease and space matter too. The numbers here come from a simple rule (more food: more animals; more predators: fewer animals), so real changes can be bigger, smaller or slower. Hungry predators may eat more of their other prey, and this model doesn't show that. Some effects really do depend.
