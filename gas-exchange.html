    Feedback

    Model limits: Real particles are far too small to see, and there are billions of them. Real air is mostly nitrogen, which is not shown. Only one alveolus is drawn; more alveoli multiply the reading. The meter works out the speed from the oxygen difference and the wall, so it is smoother than counting. In real blood, red blood cells pick up and carry the oxygen; here it just flows along with the blood.
