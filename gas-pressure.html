About this model

A real box holds billions of billions of particles, far too small to see. Real air particles move much faster, about 500 m/s. The box is flat, gravity is left out, and the temperature stays the same while you squash it. The gauge scale is made up so the starting box reads 100 kPa, about normal air pressure.

About this model

Push numbers: 100 is the push of the air outside. The can is drawn cut open so you can see inside. Real water molecules are far too small to see, and there are billions of billions of them. Some water vapour escapes from the top while the can is heated. Once crushed, the bent metal holds its new shape, so the pushes do not balance again as they do in the other models. The picture is flat and leaves out gravity for the gas.
