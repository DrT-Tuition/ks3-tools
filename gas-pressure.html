About this model

A real box holds billions of billions of particles, far too small to see. Real air particles move much faster, about 500 m/s. The box is flat, gravity is left out, and the temperature stays the same while you squash it. The gauge scale is made up so the starting box reads 100 kPa, about normal air pressure.
