About this model

Every model leaves something out. The nucleus is drawn thousands of times bigger than real: an atom’s radius is more than 10 000 times the radius of its nucleus. Electrons are drawn as dots on circles, but they don’t sit still at fixed points. The shells fill 2, 8, 8, 2: this simple rule works for the first 20 elements only. Abundances are rounded, and very rare isotopes are left out. In the alpha scattering model far more alpha particles are deflected and bounce back than in the real experiment (only about 1 in 8000 bounced back), so that you can see them, and the tracks are simplified. Real gold foil was over a thousand atoms thick.
