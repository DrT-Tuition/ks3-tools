About this model

The body model is a simple computer model, not real data. Its numbers are chosen to show the main ideas: real blood glucose changes from person to person and from day to day. Time is sped up: an hour takes a few seconds. The organs are not to scale and not in their exact places, the dots stand for far more molecules than are shown, and stores are drawn as small grains. Only a few organs are drawn, and only the liver and one muscle show stores. The glucose test values are typical textbook-style values for two people, measured in mmol/dm³ (the same as mmol/L on a blood glucose meter). A doctor or nurse plans each person’s treatment.
