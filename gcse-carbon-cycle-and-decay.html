About this model

The carbon and water cycles show the main processes only; pictures are not to scale and the decomposers are magnified. Fossil fuels formed over millions of years from dead organisms that did not decay. The compost heap uses made-up but sensible numbers (best rate 4 kg of waste per week; 12 kg of compost is left at the end): real heaps vary. In the practical, the times are model values with a little run-to-run variation, and the clock runs fast. You could measure the pH with a pH probe and data logger instead of the indicator. The full step-by-step method, with measuring the volumes and repeats, is in the Decay practical tool (link under the results table).
