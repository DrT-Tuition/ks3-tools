Compare mitosis and meiosis

About this model

The pictures are simple models, not to scale. The model cell has 2 pairs of chromosomes so you can count them; human cells have many more. In a cell that is not dividing, chromosomes are long thin threads that you cannot see with a light microscope; they are drawn short here. The colours only show which parent each chromosome came from. In the meiosis picture, sections of matching chromosomes swap ends (you do not need the details for GCSE). The 24-hour cell cycle and the root tip fields are typical values chosen to be easy to read: real cell cycles vary a lot between cell types. The phase names (prophase, metaphase, anaphase, telophase) are extra: AQA GCSE does not ask for them.
