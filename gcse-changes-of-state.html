About this model

48 molecules stand for the water; a real 0.50 kg sample has about 17 million million million million of them. The container is sealed, and the pressure is taken to stay at normal air pressure, so water melts at 0 °C and boils at 100 °C. The heater and the cooler transfer exactly 1000 J every second, and no energy is lost to the surroundings (in a real practical some is, so a measured specific latent heat comes out too high). Time is sped up. The bar shows the energy put in since the ice was at −20 °C: here all of it goes into kinetic energy on the sloping parts and into potential energy on the flat parts (a simplification). The forces between the molecules are not drawn, the molecules are not to scale, and the spaces between gas molecules are far too small.
