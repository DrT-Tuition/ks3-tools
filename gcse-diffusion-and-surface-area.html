About this model

Particles: a flat (2D) box with far fewer, far bigger particles than real; only the particles of one substance are drawn (not the water or air they move through). Each particle moves at random on a fine grid; the particles don't bump into each other. The temperature effect is made much bigger than real so you can see it. Every run of the same set-up starts from the same arrangement, so it gives the same counts; real repeats vary. Agar cubes: the times and depths are model numbers like a class result (real times depend on the agar and the alkali); the corners of a real cube go pink a little sooner than the middles of the faces. Surface area : volume: real organisms are not cubes or blocks, but the ratio changes with size and shape in the same way. Exchange surfaces: simple pictures, not to scale.
