About this model

Stores and transfers: each scene is a system. The chart shows the energy in each store; for a closed system the total stays the same (energy is conserved), and for the kettle and the phone it equals the energy supplied from the mains. Sound is counted with the energy that is dissipated. The ball and the roller coaster use a chosen fraction of energy dissipated; the mass on the spring ignores air resistance, so it would bounce for ever (a real one slowly stops); its gravitational potential energy is measured from its lowest point. The bars for the stores of a whole kettle, phone or car are simplified: real devices also store energy in other parts. Calculate: g = 9.8 N/kg. The motors lift at a steady speed; their power is the useful power output to the crate. Eₑ = ½ke² only applies while the spring is not stretched past its limit of proportionality. Sankey diagrams: drawn to scale, with typical (rounded) values; real devices vary. Pictures of the ball, phone, roller coaster car and motor are simple drawings.
