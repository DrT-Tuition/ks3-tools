About this model

The hormone levels are typical shapes for a 28-day cycle, drawn as relative levels (low to high) with no units; the four hormones are not drawn to the same scale, and real levels vary between people and from cycle to cycle. Ovulation is shown on Day 14 and implantation on Day 21. The diagrams are simplified and not to scale. The hormone map shows the main effects only; after ovulation the empty follicle (the corpus luteum) makes progesterone; it is not drawn. On Fertilisation, the cells are drawn far bigger than real and only a few of the millions of sperm are shown. The contraception list is not complete. Apart from not having sex at all, no method is 100% effective.
