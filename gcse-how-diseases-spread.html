Spread detective: how did it get there?

Model limits: Pathogens are far too small to see without a microscope: here they are drawn hugely enlarged, and only a few are shown. Viruses are much smaller than bacteria. The model shows one route at a time. In the water and food routes the pathogen is already in the water or on the food. Isolation is shown blocking the air and direct contact routes, because it keeps the infected person away from others. The other measures block only the route the lesson sorts them under. Not every bacterium makes toxins.
