    Model limits: The six unknown samples are invented: each is a solution of one salt, picked at random. The colours are drawn to match the AQA descriptions; real colours vary with how much of a substance there is and with the light. Some metal ions give no clear flame colour, so the flame stays blue. Amounts are small and not to scale, and time is sped up. The method follows the AQA required practical. These tests give observations, not measurements, so there are no means or anomalies.
