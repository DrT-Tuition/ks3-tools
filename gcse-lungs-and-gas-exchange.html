    About this model

    Breathing: a flat cut-away picture, not to scale; only quiet breathing is shown, and the meters show only lower, same or higher, with no numbers. Alveolus: one alveolus with far fewer, far bigger particles than real; nitrogen and water vapour are not shown; the meter works out the rate from the oxygen difference and the wall, so it is smoother than counting, and more alveoli multiply it; in real blood red blood cells carry the oxygen. Diffusion rate: the rule compares rates in relative units; it does not give a real rate. Diffusion is also faster at a higher temperature (the particles move faster); temperature is not in the Diffusion rate model. Surface area : volume: real organisms are not cubes, but the ratio changes with size in the same way. Exercise: the traces are simplified, with every breath the same; real breathing varies from breath to breath and from person to person.
