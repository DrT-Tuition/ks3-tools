    Model limits: Time is sped up. The cylinder size, how much copper oxide is on a spatula, how fast the water evaporates and the number of days are invented example values, and they vary a little each time. The method follows the AQA required practical. The solution becomes saturated with about half its water left (this varies a little each time); stopping with under about 15 % left counts as heated to dryness. A preparation has no repeat readings, so there are no anomalies. Real crystals vary more in size and shape than the drawings.
