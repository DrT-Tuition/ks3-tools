    Model limits: Shapes stand for binding sites and antigens: real ones are molecules, far too small to see. Not to scale. A few cells and antibodies stand for very many. The shapes, cells, numbers and scenes are made up to show the idea. Each use is simplified: no test-strip details, no named hormones or drugs. Choosing the wrong binding site for the cancer drug only shows what “specific” means.
