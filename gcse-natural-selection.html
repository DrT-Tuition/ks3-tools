    One species lives in two different places. Predators hunt in both.

    Model limits: All the numbers are invented example data. Real populations are much bigger than 20 animals. Colour is set by one gene with two alleles, and here the allele for brown is dominant: that is the model's own rule. A predator, drawn as a bird, is the only cause of death, and the chances of being eaten (50 % if easy to see, 10 % if camouflaged) are made up. One press is one generation. At least 4 animals always survive. The starting mix (2 BB, 8 Bb, 10 bb) is made up too. In Two populations only colour is drawn: real populations become different in many ways, over thousands of generations, before they are two species. The "very many generations later" jump is not to scale.
