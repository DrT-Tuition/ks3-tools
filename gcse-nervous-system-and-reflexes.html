    About this model

    The diagrams are simplified and not to scale. A real nerve holds the axons of many neurones, and many neurones work together in a reflex. The arm, bones and muscle are drawn as in Muscle Pairs; the spinal cord is shown as a cross-section, with the grey matter in the middle. The impulse is slowed right down so you can follow it, and the times in Reflex or conscious? are a model: real reflexes take tens of milliseconds, and responses that go through the brain take a few hundred.
