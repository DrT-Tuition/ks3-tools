Model limits: The masses and percentage changes are invented example data. The values are chosen to match the lesson’s class results, with random scatter (about ±1.5 %) and the odd anomaly. Real class repeats often differ by more than this, so anomalies are harder to spot; real potatoes vary, and real graphs are often curved. 30 minutes is sped up to a few seconds. In the cell picture, the particles are far too big and far too few, the numbers are rounded, and the cell wall is left out.
