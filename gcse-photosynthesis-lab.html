Safety: keep water away from the lamp and its plug, and wipe up spills (electric shock). Goggles on. An old-style lamp gets hot: let it cool before you touch it. Wash your hands after handling pondweed.

HT Higher tier only. Light intensity ∝ 1 ÷ distance².

Model limits: Rates are in arbitrary units from a simple model with the same shapes as the lesson’s graphs; real plants differ. In the Virtual RP, the bubble counts, volumes, the lamp’s warming of the water and the room light are invented example data with random scatter and the odd anomaly, not real results. One minute is sped up to 6 seconds. With the gas syringe you read the scale yourself, to the nearest 0.1 cm³ mark (the model never stops the plunger exactly half-way between two marks). The lamp is treated as a tiny point of light, so its light intensity follows the inverse square law exactly. The rate of photosynthesis does not change by the same factor as the light intensity (see the Limiting factors tab).
