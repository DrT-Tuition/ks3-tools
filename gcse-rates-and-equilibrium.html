About this model

Collisions: a 2D model with only a few particles, far bigger than real ones. In a successful collision A and B join to make one particle of the product AB; for the solid, a surface particle of A leaves the solid as AB. As reactants are used up, collisions become less frequent, so the rate falls; Refill gives a fresh mixture. Each Count for 10 s starts with a fresh mixture. While it counts, each A and B that reacts is replaced (a particle of the solid goes back in its place) and each AB is taken away soon after it is made, so the count shows the rate at the start, as if there were far more particles. The rise in speed with temperature is exaggerated so you can see it, and after each bounce a particle takes a new speed from the same spread. A catalyst really works by giving the reaction a different route (often on its surface); here it is shown as grey hexagons, and the reaction profile shows its route. The counts are random, so repeats differ a little. Reversible reactions: the thermometer readings are typical values. Equilibrium: a simple reactant ⇌ product reaction with 40 particles; time is model seconds. Le Chatelier (HT): yields are worked out for ideal gases from a 1 : 3 mix of nitrogen and hydrogen; they are close to published values. The yield graph is for a 1 : 3 mix. Each change starts from the mixture on screen: adding nitrogen or removing ammonia is done at a fixed volume, a temperature change keeps the pressure of the mixture the same, and a pressure change squeezes or expands it. The mixture starts from 10 N₂ and 30 H₂ molecules.
