Model limits: The readings, times and collision counts are invented example data from a simple model, with random scatter and the odd anomaly; they are not real results. Time is sped up. Gas anomalies are invented (a misread, or a plunger that sticks for a moment). The acid is shown as a % of an unnamed stock acid, because no concentration is given. The same mass of magnesium is used in every run. The cross times come from a simple model: time = 1200 ÷ concentration (s). The stock sodium thiosulfate is taken as 40 g/dm³ (the lesson gives no value), so the mixes are 40 to 8 g/dm³. The gas curve has one smooth shape for every run. In the Collisions tab the particles move in 2D, they are not used up when they react, and the speed rise at 40 °C is exaggerated so you can see it.
