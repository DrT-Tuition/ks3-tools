From the exam question: a student’s mean reaction time after each attempt.

Model limits: The model catcher’s readings are invented example data: scattered by a few centimetres around the deck’s class means (dominant 14 cm, non-dominant 19 cm), with the odd anomaly. The conversion row is worked out by this tool from how a ruler falls. The ruler and hands are simplified and not to scale. Your own on-screen time includes your screen’s and keyboard’s delay, and you react to a picture, not a real ruler, so keep it separate from the model data.
