About this model

Water and acid: the observations are typical of small pieces at room temperature; the temperature rises are invented values in the right order (real rises depend on the amounts used). Lithium, sodium and potassium are never added to acid in school. Displacement: the particle pictures show only a few particles, far bigger than real, and leave out the water molecules. Extraction: the method bands follow the position of carbon in the series. Heating iron oxide or zinc oxide with carbon needs a furnace, not a Bunsen burner. The aluminium cell is simplified (real cells are much bigger, run for years and are topped up with aluminium oxide). Low-grade ores: the steps are simplified, and both methods are slow.
