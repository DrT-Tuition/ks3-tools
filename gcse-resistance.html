Conclusion and evaluation

Model limits: The readings are invented example data, not real results. The model uses a 1.5 V cell with 1.2 Ω for the cell, leads and ammeter together, and a wire of 0.10 Ω per cm. Each switch-on is a fresh contact at the clips, so readings scatter a little (clip position ±0.3 cm, contact ±0.02 Ω). Each test has a 1 in 10 chance of an invented poor contact (an anomaly, 22–35 % too high), but never more than one in the same row. While the current flows the wire warms (fastest at short lengths, when the current is biggest), and its resistance changes with temperature (0.5 % per °C in this model); it cools when switched off (about 15 s to “cool”). Clip A starts on the 5 cm mark. Clip B moves in 0.5 cm steps. The meters are shown as magnified straight scales (half the range at a time on a phone). Series and parallel uses an ideal 6 V supply.
