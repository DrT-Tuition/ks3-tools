    The four steps are hidden for this question.

    Graphs: how the breed changes over the generations

    Model limits: All the numbers are invented example data: the wool, milk and egg values, the “thick wool”, “high milk” and “large eggs” lines, and the genes and alleles. Real flocks and herds are far bigger. Here the characteristic is set only by the alleles of five genes; in real animals the environment matters too. The model counts the alleles of 11 genes (37 kinds). Bulls give no milk and cockerels lay no eggs: their dashed number is the milk or egg size their alleles would give in a female (breeders use their mothers’ records). Each generation, the parents are replaced by their offspring. Nothing mutates. The small group’s family, and which animals carry the allele for the inherited defect, are the model’s own choice.
