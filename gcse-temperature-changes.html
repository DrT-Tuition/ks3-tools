Model limits: The temperatures are invented example data, shaped to the lesson’s results, with random scatter and the odd anomaly. They are not real measurements. Time is sped up. Each solution starts at room temperature. Station 1 uses the 6.5 °C rise from the lesson’s hook (neutralisation is exothermic). In the practical, the temperature falls after the highest point, as in the lesson’s results. Energy is never made or destroyed: it is transferred between the chemicals and the surroundings, and the temperature change shows that transfer. Real heat losses depend on the room, the cup and how fast you work.
