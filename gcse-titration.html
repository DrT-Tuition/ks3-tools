    Model limits: Time is sped up. The acid's concentration (new each visit), the drop size, how fast the tap runs, the small scatter in the volume of alkali measured each time and the size of each mistake's effect are invented example values. The method follows the AQA required practical. The colour change is sometimes noticed a little late, so about 1 pair of careful titrations in 4 doesn't agree closely enough and you need another one. No anomalies are added at random: a titre that doesn't fit comes from the scatter or from how the titration was done.
