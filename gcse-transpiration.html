About this model

Leaf: a flat textbook drawing, not to scale; real leaves have many more cells and stomata, and veins of many sizes. Transport: the whole plant is simplified; the arrows show directions only. Potometer: the rates are invented values that change in the same direction as real ones (each condition multiplies the rate by a fixed amount); time is speeded up, so the stopwatch runs 15 times as quickly as a real clock. A potometer measures the water taken up by the shoot, which is nearly all lost by transpiration (a little is used in photosynthesis or kept in the cells). Factors: the picture shows far fewer, far bigger water molecules than real; other gases are not shown. Stomata count: drawn fields; real counts vary from leaf to leaf, so take a mean of several fields.
