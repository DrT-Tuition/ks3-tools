Model limits:
  • Growth rates, lengths and bend sizes are made-up example values, not real data. Not to scale.
  • The dots only show which side has more auxin, not real amounts.
  • As a shoot turns to face the light (or to point up), the two sides get more equal light, so in real plants the auxin evens out and the bend slows.
  • Root auxin is shown already in the root: the model doesn't show it being made.
  • Roots respond to gravity only here. On its side, the model offers light from above or no light.
  • The root is drawn as if on damp paper so you can see it. Each dish shows 5 seedlings (the method uses 10).
  • With "Move auxin yourself", the model ignores light and gravity.
