Model limits: Real particles are far too small to see. Here there are only a few, and they vibrate more when they are hotter; they never change size or colour. The two blocks store the same energy for each degree, so they meet halfway. The pool and cup particle numbers stand in for billions. The flame is set to 100 °C. Wood is only 10 times slower than metal here; the real gap is much bigger. Convection and radiation are not shown.
