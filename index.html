Dr T’s Tuition

Biology

Breathing Breathe in and out. Watch the ribs, intercostal muscles and diaphragm move, and see why air flows in. Breathing Gas Exchange Watch oxygen and carbon dioxide diffuse in an alveolus. Change the wall, blood flow and breathing. Gas exchange Microscope Place an onion slide, choose a lens and focus with the coarse and fine knobs. Work out the magnification. Cells Muscle Pairs Bend the arm and the knee. See which muscle contracts and which relaxes. Muscles and joints Digestive System Follow a meal from mouth to anus. Tap each organ, then test which enzyme breaks down each food. Digestion Pondweed Move the lamp and count the oxygen bubbles. Find the mean and plot how light affects photosynthesis. Photosynthesis Food Web Draw the arrows, find the food chains, then remove an animal and see what happens to the rest of the web. Food webs Natural Selection Turn the bark sooty, let the birds hunt and the moths breed, and watch the peppered moth population change over generations. Natural selection

Chemistry

Molecule Builder Join atoms to build water, carbon dioxide and methane. Sort elements, compounds and mixtures, and read chemical formulae. Atoms and compounds Balance the Equation Change the big numbers until every atom is equal on both sides. Three levels, hints and your own equations. Chemical reactions Particle Model Heat and cool a substance. Watch the particles melt, boil, condense and freeze. Particles Diffusion Lift the barrier and race hot against cold. Watch gases and dye spread out. Particles Gas Pressure Pump in particles, heat them or squash them. Watch the pressure gauge. Particles Indicator Lab Test solutions with litmus, red cabbage and universal indicator. Find acid, neutral or alkali, and the pH. Acids and alkalis Neutralise It Add alkali to acid drop by drop. Watch the colour and pH reach 7, then overshoot. Match stings and cures. Acids and alkalis Acid Strength Choose a strong or weak acid and how concentrated it is. Watch the particles split up and see the pH. Acids and alkalis Naming Salts Mix an acid with a metal, a base or a carbonate. Watch what happens, then build the salt's name. Acids and alkalis Dissolving Add sugar, salt or sand to water or oil. Watch the balance and the particles, and find when it is saturated. Separating mixtures Separate It Filter, evaporate, distil and run chromatography. Get the salt out of rock salt. Separating mixtures Metal Reactions Heat metals in a flame, drop them in water or steam, then build the reactivity series from your evidence. Metals and acids Displacement Lab Put a metal in a solution. Does it push the other metal out? Use the reactivity series to predict. Metals and acids

Physics

Spring Stretch Hang masses on a spring, measure the extension and plot the graph. Find the elastic limit, and see why the floor pushes back. Forces Moon Phases Move the Moon round the Earth and see its phase from the UK. Compare the old and new models of the Universe. Space Make a Wave Shake a slinky up and down or push and pull it. Follow the red coil, label the wave and see two waves meet. Waves and sound Sound Waves Change loudness and pitch. Watch the air particles and the oscilloscope trace. Sound Echo Timer Clap, time the echo and work out how far away the cliff, seabed or moth is. Sound Light Rays Aim a ray box at a mirror or glass block. Measure angles from the normal. Light Colour Mixer Mix coloured lights, use filters and light up objects. Predict what you will see. Light Lens and Eye Focus an image with a convex lens, then label the eye and compare it with a camera. Light

Working scientifically

Graph It Fill in a results table, find the mean and the anomaly, plot the graph and draw a line of best fit. Working scientifically

A-level Biology

DNA Builder Build nucleotides, join them by condensation, then pair antiparallel strands into a double helix. OCR A 2.1.3 DNA Replication Copy DNA at a replication fork with gyrase, helicase, DNA polymerase and ligase, then test the three models with Meselson and Stahl. OCR A 2.1.3 Action Potential Stimulate an axon membrane, watch the Na⁺ and K⁺ channels and the trace, then race myelinated and unmyelinated axons. OCR A 5.1.3 Protein Synthesis Transcribe a gene into mRNA in the nucleus, then translate it codon by codon at a ribosome. Explore the genetic code and mutations. OCR A 2.1.3
