Free interactive tools for KS3 science lessons. Open one on the board or share the link. No log-in, nothing to install.

Dr T’s Tuition

Biology

Breathing Breathe in and out. Watch the ribs, intercostal muscles and diaphragm move, and see why air flows in. Breathing Gas Exchange Watch oxygen and carbon dioxide diffuse in an alveolus. Change the wall, blood flow and breathing. Gas exchange Muscle Pairs Bend the arm and the knee. See which muscle contracts and which relaxes. Muscles and joints

Chemistry

Balance the Equation Change the big numbers until every atom is equal on both sides. Three levels, hints and your own equations. Chemical reactions Particle Model Heat and cool a substance. Watch the particles melt, boil, condense and freeze. Particles Diffusion Lift the barrier and race hot against cold. Watch gases and dye spread out. Particles Gas Pressure Pump in particles, heat them or squash them. Watch the pressure gauge. Particles

Physics

Make a Wave Shake a slinky up and down or push and pull it. Follow the red coil, label the wave and see two waves meet. Waves and sound Sound Waves Change loudness and pitch. Watch the air particles and the oscilloscope trace. Sound Echo Timer Clap, time the echo and work out how far away the cliff, seabed or moth is. Sound Light Rays Aim a ray box at a mirror or glass block. Measure angles from the normal. Light Colour Mixer Mix coloured lights, use filters and light up objects. Predict what you will see. Light Lens and Eye Focus an image with a convex lens, then label the eye and compare it with a camera. Light
