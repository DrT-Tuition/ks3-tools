KS3 Physics

Years 7–9. Interactive physics tools.

Dr T’s Classroom

P1.1 Forces

Balanced Forces Run a tug of war, drive a rocket car with a parachute, and decide which forces are balanced in the forces circus. Spring Stretch Hang masses on a spring, measure the extension and plot the graph. Find the elastic limit, and see why the floor pushes back.

P1.2 Sound

Echo Timer Clap, time the echo and work out how far away the cliff, seabed or moth is. Make a Wave Shake a slinky up and down or push and pull it. Follow the red coil, label the wave and see two waves meet. Sound Waves Change loudness and pitch. Watch the air particles and the oscilloscope trace.

P1.3 Light

Colour Mixer Mix coloured lights, use filters and light up objects. Predict what you will see. Lens and Eye Focus an image with a convex lens, then label the eye and compare it with a camera. Light Rays Aim a ray box at a mirror or glass block. Measure angles from the normal.

P1.4 Space

Moon Phases Move the Moon round the Earth and see its phase from the UK. Compare the old and new models of the Universe.

P2.1 Electricity and magnetism

Circuit Builder Build series and parallel circuits. Add cells and bulbs, then read the ammeters and voltmeters. Electromagnet Change the turns, current and core. Count the paper clips and record a fair test. Static Charge Rub a balloon on a jumper and count the electrons that move. Then see how charged balloons push and pull.

P2.2 Energy

Heat Flow Touch a hot block to a cool one and watch them reach the same temperature. Compare a pool and a cup, then metal and wood. Skate Ramp Let a skater roll down a ramp. Watch the energy move between the stores, then switch friction on and see where it goes.

P2.3 Motion and pressure

Motion Graphs Work out speeds, time a trolley down a ramp, then drive a walker and watch the distance–time graph draw itself. Match a graph and read the speed from it. Pressure Lab Sink blocks and people into sand and mud to see how area changes pressure. Then lower a sensor into water, pull the tape off a bottle of jets and explore upthrust. Seesaw Balance a seesaw, turn a nut with a spanner and lift a load with a lever. Work out each moment and predict which way it tips.

Working scientifically

Graph It Fill in a results table, find the mean and the anomaly, plot the graph and draw a line of best fit.
