KS4 Biology

GCSE, Years 10–11. Interactive biology tools.

Dr T’s Classroom
BiologyChemistryPhysics

4.1.1 Cell structure

Microbiology Practical Required practical (Biology only, separate Biology RP2, Higher): use aseptic technique, measure zones of inhibition, work out their areas and model bacterial growth. Microscope Lab Required practical (Combined RP1 · Biology RP1): make a slide, focus a light microscope, draw and measure cells, and calculate magnification step by step.

4.1.3 Transport in cells

Osmosis Practical Required practical (Combined RP2 · Biology RP3): cut and weigh potato cylinders, calculate percentage change in mass, plot the results and find where the line crosses 0 %.

4.2.2 Animal tissues, organs and organ systems

Enzymes and pH Required practical (Combined RP4 · Biology RP5): time how long amylase takes to digest starch at each pH, work out the rate and find the optimum. Food Tests Required practical (Combined RP3 · Biology RP4): prepare food samples and test for starch, sugar, protein and lipid safely.

4.3.1 Communicable disease

How Diseases Spread Pick a pathogen and a route, watch it pass from person to person, then try to block it.

4.3.2 Monoclonal antibodies

Monoclonal Antibody Maker Make monoclonal antibodies step by step, target them to one antigen, and weigh up their uses.

4.4.1 Photosynthesis

Photosynthesis Lab Required practical (Combined RP5 · Biology RP6): find what limits the rate of photosynthesis, then run the pondweed practical and plot the results.

4.4.2 Respiration

Exercise and the Body Make the body work harder, watch heart, lungs and muscles respond, and see what happens when oxygen runs short.

4.5.2 The human nervous system

Reaction Time Required practical (Combined RP6 · Biology RP7): run the ruler-drop test, find means, convert to reaction times and compare hands.

4.5.4 Plant hormones

Tropism Simulator Required practical (Biology only, separate Biology RP8): change the light and gravity, watch where auxin goes, and see shoots and roots bend. Then run the RP8 dishes.

4.6.1 Reproduction and inheritance

DNA to Protein Read a gene in triplets, build the protein, then change one base and see what the mutation does. Punnett Square Builder Put each parent's alleles into gametes, fill the Punnett square, then read off the ratios and chances.

4.6.2 Variation and evolution

Natural Selection Change the environment and watch a population of beetles or rabbits evolve over generations. Selective Breeding Choose the best male and female, breed them, pick the best offspring and repeat, then see the risks of inbreeding.

4.7.2 Ecosystems and biodiversity

Predator–Prey Cycles Build a food chain, then watch rabbit and fox numbers rise and fall in cycles. Quadrat Sampling Required practical (Combined RP7 · Biology RP9): sample a field with random quadrats and a transect, then estimate the population size.
