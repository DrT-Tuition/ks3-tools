Model limits: The lens is drawn as if it bends light once, at its middle. In the eye, the cornea and the lens both bend light; here only the lens is shown bending it. Only rays from the flame tip are drawn, but every point on the candle sends out light. The candle stops at 14 cm so the image stays on the bench. Closer than F (10 cm), no image forms on a screen. Drawings are not to scale.
