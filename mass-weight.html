Model limits: g values are rounded: Earth 10 N/kg, Moon 1.6 N/kg. g is the pull at the surface: it gets weaker further away. A top-pan balance really measures a force and is set for Earth, so this model uses a beam balance that compares masses. Extension places use standard values; Jupiter has no solid ground.
