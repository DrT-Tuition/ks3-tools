About this model

Days are typical: real cycles vary. The diagram is simplified and not to scale. It shows one egg from one ovary, and the embryo implanting on Day 21. On Fertilisation, the cells are drawn far bigger than real, only a few of the millions of sperm are shown, and the steps are shown in order (fertilisation on Day 14, implantation on Day 21).
