Model limits: Real cells are far smaller than they look here. The brown colour comes from the iodine stain. Real microscopes differ: this one has ×4, ×10 and ×40 objective lenses and a ×10 eyepiece. Light microscopes can magnify up to about ×2000. Here the focus moves in set steps; a real knob turns smoothly.
