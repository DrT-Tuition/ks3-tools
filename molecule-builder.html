About this model

Atoms are not to scale, and real molecules are 3D. Joins are drawn as sticks; in the Sort boxes, joined atoms touch. Each atom can make a set number of joins: H 1, O 2, N 3, C 4, Cl 1. Colours are only a guide: real atoms have no colour. Metal compounds form giant structures, not molecules.
