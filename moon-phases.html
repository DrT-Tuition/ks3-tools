About this model

Not to scale: the Sun is much bigger and further away, and the Moon is much further from the Earth than shown. The model uses exactly 28 days; the real orbit takes about 28 days. Phases are shown as seen from the UK: waxing is lit on the right. In the southern hemisphere the picture is flipped. The dark part of the Moon is drawn faintly so you can find it; in the sky you can't see it. The Earth's shadow does not cause phases: it only falls on the Moon in a lunar eclipse, which this model leaves out. Changing ideas shows only a few planets, drawn simply, and the speeds are not real.
