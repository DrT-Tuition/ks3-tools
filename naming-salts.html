Model limits: Real reactions can be slow. Copper oxide needs warm acid, as in the practical. Only some metals react safely with acid. Here the acid is always in excess, so all the solid is used up. Only the blue of copper salts is shown. Some other salts, such as iron salts, are coloured too, but here they are drawn colourless. Calcium is not offered with sulfuric acid: that reaction soon stops. The salt stays dissolved: crystals form only when the water evaporates. Names only, no formulae.

    Ending rule: the acid names the salt

    hydrochloric acid → …chloride

    sulfuric acid → …sulfate

    nitric acid → …nitrate

    The first word comes from the metal.

    Product rule

    metal + acid → salt + hydrogen

    base + acid → salt + water

    carbonate + acid → salt + water + carbon dioxide
