    Model limits: Real populations are huge: this tree shows just 24 moths. Birds are not the only cause of death. This tree always keeps at least one moth of each colour; in the real population, pale moths became rare but never died out. Colour inheritance is simplified: the survivors share the babies about equally, and each baby has its parent's colour. Real moths get genes from two parents. The real change took many years, over many generations. The chances of being eaten are the model's own numbers.
