Model limits: Real particles are far too small to see, and there are billions of them, not 48. Here every substance has the same round particles; real ones differ. The lid keeps every particle in. We pretend the pressure stays like normal air, so each substance melts and boils at its usual temperature. The forces between particles are not drawn. The heating graph is not to scale.
