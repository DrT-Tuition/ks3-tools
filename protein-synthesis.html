PhosphateSugar (deoxyribose in DNA, ribose in RNA)Bases: purines (A, G) double ring, pyrimidines (C, T, U) single ringAmino acid (three-letter name)

Purines have a double ring: adenine (A) and guanine (G).

Pyrimidines have a single ring: cytosine (C), thymine (T, DNA only) and uracil (U, RNA only).

DNA with DNA: A–T and G–C.

RNA with DNA or RNA: U opposite A, A opposite T (or U), G–C. RNA has no thymine.

Codon (mRNA) and anticodon (tRNA) pair the same way, antiparallel: 5′–AUG–3′ pairs with 3′–UAC–5′.
