Model limits: This is a model. A real skater has thousands of joules, so the top is scaled to 100 J. Only three stores are drawn. The ramp is smooth and the skater is not pushed. Real friction cannot be switched off, so a real skater always stops in the end. The thermal store really spreads into the track, the wheels and the air; here it is one bar.
