People can hear from about 20 Hz to 20 000 Hz. This slider only goes from 100 Hz to 1000 Hz.

Sound plays the real pitch. It is quiet, but check your volume first.

Model limits: Real air particles are far too small to see, and there are billions of them. The motion is slowed down a lot so you can see it. Real sound spreads out in all directions. Sound is a longitudinal wave: the wavy trace is just a picture of the air pressure going up and down at the ear. Across the trace is time. The waves are not drawn to scale.
